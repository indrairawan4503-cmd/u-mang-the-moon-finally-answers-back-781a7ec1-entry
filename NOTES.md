# NOTES — The Moon Finally Answers Back, variant A (WISHES)

Angle: the Moon is tired of wishes and returns them — postage due,
unopened — and the one wish it did grant literally (a pony) is now a
permanent, rent-eating problem in the wisher's kitchen.

## Versions

- **v1 — 171 chars:** "Four billion wishes, and not one of you ever wished ME anything. So I'm returning them all — postage due, unopened. The pony one? I granted it. It's in your kitchen now."
  Baseline. Grievance + return + literal grant all present, but the ending is flat ("It's in your kitchen now" states a fact, no bite) and 171 chars wastes budget.
- **v2 — 197 chars:** "…The pony one? I granted that one. He's in your kitchen now, and he isn't leaving."
  Ending sharpened: the pony doesn't just sit there, he isn't leaving — the literal grant becomes an ongoing consequence, the Moon's revenge.
- **v3 — 221 chars:** "…He's in your kitchen now, eating your rent money, and he isn't leaving."
  The pony now eats the rent money — a concrete, ongoing cost instead of a vague presence. The literal grant bites harder.
- **v4 — 221 chars:** "…The pony one? Granted. He's in your kitchen now, eating your rent money, and he isn't leaving. Good luck."
  Tightened "I granted that one" to one dry word, "Granted.", and spent the saved chars on a final Moon shrug — "Good luck." The line ends on the Moon washing its hands of the mess.
- **v5 — 217 chars (FINAL):** "…and not one of you wished ME anything. …and he isn't leaving — good luck."
  Dropped the filler "ever" and joined the sign-off to the kicker with an em-dash — one breath, one punch. No wasted words.

## FINAL

Four billion wishes, and not one of you wished ME anything. So I'm returning them all — postage due, unopened. The pony one? Granted. He's in your kitchen now, eating your rent money, and he isn't leaving — good luck.
